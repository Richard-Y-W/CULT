# Methodology change control

An official calculation change requires a proposal, empirical reason, deterministic fixtures, old/new comparison, methodology version bump, effective UTC timestamp, and a declared historical policy. Approval is represented by a migration/config change reviewed with the code.

Published history is never silently overwritten. A correction creates an auditable revision containing original value, revision number, reason, actor/process, and revision timestamp. Registry aggregation changes, expression merges/splits, source eligibility changes, calibration changes, and close rules are methodology changes—not ordinary refactors.

Research variants may coexist without becoming official. Negative or inconclusive comparisons are retained.
