# Product vision

## We trade how the internet expresses itself

Most trend products ask what the internet is talking about. CULT asks how the included public online environments express themselves. “AI” is topic attention; “we’re cooked,” `😭`, or `bruh` are expression signals. The long-term data model is Expression × Time × Platform × Context × Semantic State.

The exchange is the approachable consumer layer. Cultural indexes are the analytical layer. A reproducible expression dataset—the CULT Observable Internet Panel—is the potential durable asset. CULT never claims that a non-probability sample of public online speech represents humanity.

The product takes engineering and data seriously and presents extremely unserious underlying assets with deadpan restraint.
