# Casual and Analyst modes

**Five seconds to understand. Five hours to explore.**

Casual Mode defaults to asset cards, plain charts, obvious BUY and SHORT actions, portfolio marks, indexes, movers, and short market notes. It says: “I think 😭 is going up.” It avoids unexplained quant vocabulary and casino mechanics.

Analyst Mode at `/terminal` is a restrained, keyboard-ready research surface. It exposes the same assets through platform shares, semantic composition, entropy, momentum, realized volatility, correlations, pair ratios, and reproducible backtests. Its humor comes from institutional seriousness, not imitation of any proprietary terminal.

Free users should eventually see selected advanced readings. The ANALYST tier expands depth; it does not make the simulated leaderboard pay-to-win.
